[h1]Men-At-Arms Manager[/h1]

[b]Efficiently manage and reorganize your Men-At-Arms with an intuitive interface.[/b]

[h2]Overview[/h2]
The Men-At-Arms Manager adds an interface to your military screen for reorganizing and retraining both personal and title Men-at-Arms. Use the same familiar controls for your own troops and your Provincial or Thematic Armies.

[h2]Key Features[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Transfer Soldiers[/b] - Move individual soldiers between regiments of the same type
[*] [b]Transfer Regiments[/b] - Move entire stacks between compatible regiments
[*] [b]Title Men-at-Arms[/b] - Manage Provincial and Thematic Armies in a separate mode. Transfers stay within the same owning title; personal and title troops never mix
[*] [b]Retrain Men-at-Arms[/b] - Convert regiments to eligible unit types using the existing retrain pricing, with Treasury payment where applicable
[*] [b]Culture-Specific Artwork[/b] - Regiment cards use the appropriate cultural illustrations, matching the game
[*] [b]Military Screen Integration[/b] - Open personal or title management directly from the corresponding military tab (F3)
[*] [b]Multi-Language Support[/b] - Full localization in English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Chinese, and Korean
[/list]

[h2]How It Works[/h2]
[olist]
[*] Open your Military screen (F3)
[*] For personal troops, click the manager button in [b]Your Armies[/b]. For title troops, use the manager button in [b]Provincial Armies[/b] or [b]Thematic Armies[/b]
[*] Select source and target regiments
[*] Transfer soldiers or stacks between compatible regiments, or use the [b]Retrain[/b] tab to choose a new unit type
[*] Confirm your changes or adjust as needed
[/olist]

[h2]Requirements[/h2]
[list]
[*] Men-At-Arms must be lowered (not raised/deployed)
[*] Cannot be used during wartime
[*] Loaned-out title troops can be viewed but cannot be modified
[*] Title retraining requires an unstationed regiment and native permission to recruit its replacement, including a free regiment slot
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
Supports CK3 1.20.*. The mod overrides the military window; other mods that replace the same window may require a compatibility patch.
