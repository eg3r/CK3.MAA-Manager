[h1]Men-at-Arms Manager: Reworked[/h1]

[b]A complete rework of my [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3580440930]Men-at-Arms Manager[/url].[/b] Everything now happens right in the Military window (F3) and the game's own recruitment window.

[h2]Modded Men-at-Arms now work![/h2]
[b]Mods that add new Men-at-Arms types are fully supported.[/b] Retraining uses the game's own recruitment window and reads your real prices, so every type you can recruit shows up with the correct cost.

[h2]Large armies made easy[/h2]
[b]Handling dozens of Men-at-Arms regiments is now far easier.[/b] Show them as a compact [b]list[/b], [b]grouped by type[/b] with folding groups, or as [b]tiles[/b] like the base game; your choice is remembered.

[h2]Features[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Move stacks and soldiers[/b] between regiments of the same type, instantly, with undo.
[*][b]Retrain in three clicks[/b]: size and soldiers carry over, the exact fee is shown first.
[*][b]Quartermaster tips[/b] for regiments that can grow or be combined.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Works at peace with no armies raised; pause the game to retrain.
[*]Replaces the Military and recruitment windows: mods that change them will conflict.
[*]CK3 1.20.* · English, French, German, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Chinese, Korean.
[/list]
